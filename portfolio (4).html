Case Study — ESOP Dashboard

Making equity
understandable.

Lead Product Designer
4 months
Web App (B2B SaaS)

Overview

ESOP Dashboard is an equity management platform for startups and HR teams. Employees needed a clear, self-serve way to understand their stock options, vesting schedules, and tax implications — all in one place.

Problem

HR teams were fielding hundreds of repetitive queries about equity every month. Employees couldn't understand their ESOP letters. The existing tool was a glorified spreadsheet with no contextual guidance, resulting in confusion, distrust, and low engagement.

Research Insights

68% of employees didn't know how much their options were currently worth
HR teams spent ~6 hrs/week answering equity questions that could be self-served
Users needed progressive disclosure — summary first, details on demand
Mobile access was critical: employees checked equity data during appraisal periods

Case Study — UPI ATM

Cash, without the card.

UX Designer
6 weeks
Mobile App (Android + iOS)

Overview

UPI ATM enables cardless cash withdrawals by linking a user's UPI ID to a physical ATM interaction. This case study covers the complete flow — from initiating a withdrawal in-app to collecting cash at the ATM.

Problem

Users frequently arrived at ATMs without debit cards — especially younger demographics who rely on UPI for daily transactions. Existing cardless options required bank-specific apps with cumbersome flows that had high drop-off rates.

Research Insights

1 in 3 users had missed a cardless ATM opportunity due to confusing flows
Time pressure at ATMs created anxiety — the flow needed to be under 30 seconds
OTP-based confirmations were the most trusted security pattern
Users expected clear session expiry feedback to avoid failed withdrawals

Case Study — Schooldex

One platform for
every classroom need.

Lead UX/UI + Design System
8 months
Web + Tablet (EdTech SaaS)

Overview

Schooldex is a unified School Information System (SIS) and Learning Management System (LMS). I designed the end-to-end platform from scratch — building the design language, component library, and all major user flows for administrators, teachers, students, and parents.

Problem

Schools operated across fragmented tools — one for attendance, another for grades, a third for curriculum. This created data silos, duplication, and high cognitive load for teachers who managed up to 5 different tools per day.

Research Insights

Teachers averaged 4.8 context switches per hour across disconnected tools
Parents wanted real-time updates, not periodic PDF reports
A scalable design system was critical to ship fast and stay consistent across 40+ screens
Offline mode was essential for schools in low-connectivity regions
